YAKA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

YAKA is a food delivery application that allows customers to view available food items, place orders, save delivery addresses and receive order updates. This policy explains how YAKA handles personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, registered phone number and other details you provide when registering or managing your account.
  • Location and delivery information: Delivery address, landmark and, when you grant permission, device location coordinates used to help identify your delivery location.
  • Order information: Items ordered, order total, order status, delivery details and order history.
  • Saved information: Addresses you choose to save in your account.
  • Notification information: Push notification token used to send relevant order updates, where notifications are enabled.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process orders and cash-on-delivery payments.
  • Arrange deliveries and communicate order status.
  • Save delivery addresses when requested.
  • Send order-related notifications.
  • Protect the app and resolve customer support issues.

3. Location Permission

YAKA may request location permission to help fill in your current delivery location. You can deny permission and enter your address manually. Location information may be used by the store and assigned delivery partner to fulfil your order.

4. Sharing of Information

Information needed to fulfil an order may be shared with the store and assigned delivery partner. YAKA uses Supabase for backend services, which may process account, order and related information on our behalf. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the rights and security of users and the service.

5. Payments

YAKA currently supports cash on delivery. The app does not require customers to enter card details to place a cash-on-delivery order.

6. Data Security and Retention

We use access controls and database security policies intended to protect personal information. Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the service, fulfil orders, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements.

7. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your YAKA account from the account deletion option in the app or through our account deletion webpage. Active orders must be delivered or cancelled before account deletion can be completed. Some order records may be retained in anonymized form where necessary.

8. Children's Privacy

YAKA is a general food delivery service and is not specifically designed for children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our app or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this webpage with its updated date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the YAKA developer using the support contact provided on our Google Play store listing.
