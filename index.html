YAKA Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your YAKA food delivery account and associated personal information using the form below.

Request Account Deletion

How to request deletion

  1. Open the account deletion request form.
  2. Enter the phone number registered to your YAKA account.
  3. Submit your request and complete any identity verification we require.

What happens next?

We will review your request and may contact you to verify account ownership. You must have no active orders; pending orders must be delivered or cancelled before deletion can proceed.

Once deletion is completed, your account access and associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized as applicable. Certain order records may be retained in anonymized form where necessary for legitimate operational or legal requirements.

Need help?

If you have questions about your deletion request, use the contact information provided in the YAKA app or on its official store listing.
